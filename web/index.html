Why a flight recorder?

Because “sent” is not the end of the story.

Real systems fail in the space between an action and its proof. BLACKBOX keeps those states separate.

  1. AttemptServer returned 500

    The action stayed uncertain—not complete.

  2. Public checkExact record absent

    No evidence was promoted without observation.

  3. Root causeStorage permission failure

    The venue was repaired before recovery.

  4. Reviewed retrySame signed operation

    No blind retry and no rewritten signed fields.

  5. ResultPublic record verified

    The evidence—not the attempt—closed the step.
