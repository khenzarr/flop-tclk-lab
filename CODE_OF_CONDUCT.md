# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, appearance, ability, experience, education, nationality, beliefs, or personal circumstances.

## Our standards

Positive participation includes:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect;
- welcoming different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm;
- focusing on the health of the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, threats, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person’s private information without permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this Code of Conduct, and may take fair corrective action when behavior is threatening, harmful, or disruptive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer’s GitHub profile, [@khenzarr](https://github.com/khenzarr). Do not expose private personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly, and maintainers will protect the reporter’s privacy and security as far as reasonably possible.

Consequences will be proportional to community impact and may include a private correction, a formal warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. Enforcement decisions should explain the problematic behavior, set clear boundaries, and avoid unnecessary public disclosure.
